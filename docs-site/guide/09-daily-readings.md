# 📖 Daily Readings

Daily Readings are a curated collection of recovery reflections — one per day, drawn from the tradition that matches your path. They are shared, publicly-sourced content and are **not encrypted**.

## The Seven Modalities

MRT supports seven reading traditions. You can enable any combination in your preferences:

| Modality | Style |
| :--- | :--- |
| **12-Step (AA-inspired)** | Sobriety-focused, Higher Power language |
| **12-Step (NA-inspired)** | Substance-neutral, fellowship-centred |
| **12-Step (CA-inspired)** | Cocaine Anonymous tradition |
| **Recovery Dharma** | Buddhist-inspired, non-theistic |
| **SMART Recovery** | Science-based, CBT / REBT tools |
| **Secular / Stoic** | Philosophy-driven, no spiritual language |
| **Mindfulness / Buddhist** | Meditation and present-moment awareness |

## How Rotation Works

MRT picks today's reading automatically using the day of the year — the same date always returns the same reading for a given modality. There is no manual selection required. If you have multiple modalities enabled, MRT opens on your active modality for the day; swipe left or right inside the modal to browse the others.

## Accessing Your Reading

Tap **Daily Reading** on the Dynamic Anchor widget at the top of the Dashboard. The reading opens in a full-screen modal showing:
* **Theme** — the day's headline.
* **Body** — the reflection passage.
* **Reflection** — a guided question to sit with.
* **Affirmation** — a closing statement to carry through the day.
* **Go Deeper** — an optional link to further reading.

Tap the chevron (▾) next to the Daily Reading button to open an external fellowship reading instead (AA, NA, SMART, etc.) in your browser.

## Changing Your Modality Preferences

Go to **Profile → General**, scroll to the **Daily Reading** section to enable or disable individual modalities. Your selections are saved to your account and sync across devices.

## Sharing a Reading

Tap **Share reading** on any reading card. The app copies the reading's theme, body, reflection, and affirmation to your clipboard — or opens the native share sheet on supported devices.

> **Privacy note:** The share output contains only the published reading text and the MRT website URL. No personal data, journal content, or account information is ever included.

## Journal From a Reading

Inside the reading modal, tap **"Journal on this"** to open a pre-filled journal entry seeded with the reading's reflection prompt. This entry goes through the full Zero-Knowledge encryption pipeline — your response is private even though the reading itself is not.
