# 🎨 Brand Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0
**Brand Name:** My Recovery Toolkit (MRT)
**Tagline:** "The safest place to do the hardest work."

## 1. Tone of Voice
Our communication style must reflect the sensitive nature of our users' journeys (referencing `PERSONAS.md`).
* **Empathetic, not Pitying:** We understand the struggle, but we speak to the user's strength and resilience.
* **Clear & Direct:** Users in crisis ("David") cannot process complex jargon. Use short, actionable sentences.
* **Authoritative, not Clinical:** We are a secure digital companion, not a medical facility. Avoid dispensing medical advice. Always default to the "Experience, Strength, and Hope" model.
* **Privacy-First:** Every piece of marketing should reinforce our Zero-Knowledge architecture. Trust is our primary product.

## 2. Visual Identity (Vibrant Momentum)
Recovery is often painted in dreary, clinical grays. MRT uses **"Vibrant Momentum"** to make recovery feel like a high-performance, optimistic lifestyle.
* **Primary Gradients:** High-saturation glassmorphism.
* **Module Mapping:**
    * *Dashboard:* Sky to Blue (Hope)
    * *Journal/Vault:* Indigo to Violet (Deep Reflection)
    * *Habits:* Cyan to Teal (Action)
    * *Vitality:* Orange to Rose (Physical warmth)
    * *Wisdom:* Emerald to Lime (Growth)

## 3. Core Messaging Pillars
1. **Absolute Privacy:** "Your device holds the only key. We couldn't read your journal even if we wanted to."
2. **Actionable Insights:** "Don't just track days. Track patterns."
3. **Graceful Gamification:** "Smart resets mean no guilt, no schedule debt, just progress."
