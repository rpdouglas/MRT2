# 🛑 12-Step Marketing Compliance Guidelines

**Purpose:** To successfully market MRT to the recovery community, we must fiercely respect the 12 Traditions. We are a tool, not a replacement for human connection.

## 1. Non-Affiliation (Tradition 6)
* **Rule:** We are an "outside enterprise."
* **DO:** Market as a "companion tool," "personal toolkit," or "safe space for step-work."
* **DON'T:** Use terms like "The AA App," or utilize trademarked symbols (circle/triangle).

## 2. Attraction Rather Than Promotion (Tradition 11)
* **Rule:** No hard sells, no magic cures.
* **DO:** Tell stories. Use the David, Ned, Lisa, and Walt personas to show *how* the tool is used.
* **DON'T:** Make promises like "Guaranteed to keep you sober."

## 3. Redefining the "Digital Sponsor" (Tradition 8 & 12)
* **Rule:** Sponsorship is a sacred, human-to-human spiritual connection. 
* **DO:** Rebrand AI features as "Reflection Guide," "Pattern Finder," or "Insight Engine." Frame it as a tool to prepare for conversations with a human sponsor.
* **DON'T:** Never use the word "Sponsor" to describe the AI. 

## 4. Protecting Anonymity (Tradition 11 & 12)
* **Rule:** Privacy is paramount.
* **DO:** Heavily market the Zero-Knowledge "Vault", PIN rotation, and data sovereignty. Use avatars for personas.
* **DON'T:** Solicit or use real faces/names for marketing testimonials.

## 5. Staying Out of Outside Issues (Tradition 10)
* **Rule:** MRT is a neutral vessel.
* **DO:** Frame features like Breathwork as "tools for vitality."
* **DON'T:** Take stances on MAT, specific psychologies, or harm-reduction debates.
