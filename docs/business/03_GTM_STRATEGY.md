# 🚀 Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy

**Version:** 1.0
**Model:** Freemium (Free core tools, Paid AI/Export features).

## 1. Content Pillars
All marketing and social content should fall into one of three buckets:
1. **Privacy in Tech:** Educating users on why Zero-Knowledge encryption matters in health apps (Contrasting MRT against data-mining competitors).
2. **The "How-To" of Recovery:** Visualizing CBT techniques, 12-Step mechanics, and Breathwork using the app's UI.
3. **Building in Public (Dev Logs):** Showing the human side of the app. Documenting the coding journey, bug squashing, and architectural decisions.

## 2. Platform Strategy
* **YouTube (The Educational Engine):** * *Format:* Long-form screen recordings (like Notebook LM overviews), deep feature tutorials, and Dev Logs.
    * *Goal:* SEO dominance and long-term authority building.
* **TikTok / Instagram Reels (The Discovery Funnel):**
    * *Format:* 7-15 second aesthetic clips. E.g., The Vitality breathing ring expanding, or the Dashboard XP counter leveling up, paired with trending audio and our `slogans.ts` text.
    * *Goal:* High-volume top-of-funnel traffic.
* **Reddit (The Community Ground):**
    * *Format:* Genuine, text-based participation from the Founder's account in `r/stopdrinking`, `r/recovery`, and `r/SaaS`.
    * *Rule:* Never spam links. Provide massive value in comments, and only link the app when it perfectly solves the OP's specific problem.

## 3. The "Link Tree" Architecture
Because social platforms limit outbound links, we will build a native `/links` route within MRT (e.g., `www.myrecoverytoolkit.ca/links`) instead of paying for Linktree.
* **Buttons:**
    1. Open App (Dashboard)
    2. Install Guide (iOS/Android)
    3. Watch the Video (YouTube)
    4. Contact Support
