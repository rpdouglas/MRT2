## The one-sentence pitch

**Insights is where every pattern MRT's AI ever noticed lives in one place — and Recovery Capital is a real, research-grounded way to see the whole shape of your recovery, not just your sobriety count.**

---

## Why this matters in recovery

- **"Recovery Capital" is a real, established concept, not a made-up name.** It's a recognized framework (grounded in SAMHSA's own definition of recovery) measuring the actual resources that support someone staying well — not just abstinence, but the health, stability, purpose, and community around it. MRT's four domains — Health, Home, Purpose, Community — map directly onto that real framework.
- **Recovery isn't one number.** A sobriety counter tells you how long; Recovery Capital tells you *how well-supported* that recovery actually is right now, across the parts of life that hold it up.
- **Seeing the AI's own reasoning gathered in one place matters for trust.** Someone who's had an insight generated from a journal entry, a workbook session, or a Recovery Capital check-in shouldn't have to remember which screen it came from — Insights is that one place, plain and searchable.
- **Longitudinal tracking (not just a single score) is what makes a pattern visible.** One check-in is a snapshot; a year of them is a trend — the same reasoning behind Journal's own long-horizon Deep Dive analysis.

**Marketing framing:** "not just how long — how well" — Recovery Capital as the missing second half of the sobriety counter, backed by a real framework, not a wellness-app invention.

## What Insights & Recovery Capital actually does

### Insights Log — every AI insight, one place
A single, filterable timeline of everything MRT's AI has ever generated for someone — pattern analyses from Journal, workbook analyses, all in one reverse-chronological list. Anything actionable inside an insight converts to a task in one tap, the same consistent pattern used everywhere else in the app.

### Recovery Capital — four real domains, tracked over time
A short check-in (five questions: Health, Home, Purpose, Community, and overall Resilience) produces a score across four domains, out of 10 each, 40 total. Free tier gets an honest self-report score once a month; Premium adds an AI-augmented score that reads recent journal entries for real evidence behind the number, plus a written narrative — not just a number, but a few sentences of what the AI actually noticed, on a weekly cadence.

### Three ways to see it
- **Snapshot** — the most recent check-in, at a glance, with the change from last time.
- **Trends** — every check-in ever taken, charted over time — the whole shape of someone's recovery capital, not just where it stands today.
- **History** — every past check-in, each one expandable into its own story (for the periods with a full AI narrative attached).

### A trust detail worth using in copy
If someone had Premium when they did a check-in, that period's full AI narrative stays theirs to read back — even if they're not on Premium anymore later. Nothing already written gets locked away by a tier change.

## How each persona uses Insights & Recovery Capital

MRT designs around six real recovery personas (full detail in `docs/PERSONAS.md`). Do not invent details beyond what's here or in the personas doc.

### Walt — "The Zen Master" (35+ years, AA-origin, now Recovery Dharma)
> *"Recovery is a lifelong practice of mindfulness and reflection."*

Trends is Walt's screen. He's not interested in one score on one day — he wants the whole longitudinal shape, and that's exactly what the trend view gives him: every check-in ever taken, charted, not just the latest.

**Marketing angle:** the shape of decades, not a single data point.

### Maya — "The Systematiser" (6–18 months, SMART Recovery / secular / CBT)
> *"I want to understand the mechanics of my brain so I can upgrade my operating system."*

History is Maya's screen — each past assessment shows exactly how many of her own journal entries actually fed that score ("Based on N journal entries"), the same traceability standard she holds every AI feature in the app to. She won't trust a number she can't audit, and this screen is built to be audited.

**Marketing angle:** every score, traceable back to real evidence — never a black box.

### Lisa — "The Service Superstar" (7 years, Alcoholics Anonymous, sponsors 3–6 women)
> *"I have five sponsees and a full-time job. I need to stay organized so I don't burn out."*

The check-in itself is designed as a self-care practice for Lisa specifically — a short, structured moment to check on her own Health/Home/Purpose/Community, the same counterbalance role Vitality plays for her elsewhere in the app (`docs/marketing/vitality.md`).

**Marketing angle:** five questions, once in a while, about her own life — not her sponsees'.

### David, Ned, and Jordan
No dedicated story here — Recovery Capital isn't built around a specific moment for David (crisis), Ned (momentum), or Jordan (stabilization). Don't force one; their stories belong to Tools, Games, and Tasks respectively.

## How Insights & Recovery Capital connects to the rest of MRT

- **Every source of AI analysis lands here** — Journal's AI Pattern Analysis, Workbook analysis, and Recovery Capital's own AI narrative all feed the same Insights Log.
- **Suggested actions become Tasks**, the same one-tap pattern used across Journal, Workbooks, and everywhere else AI generates a recommendation.
- **Recovery Capital shares the same encryption model** — the score itself is visible at a glance (so it can be charted without decryption), while the AI's actual written reasoning stays encrypted until someone chooses to read it.

## Brand voice & marketing guardrails

Everything from `docs/marketing/journal.md`'s guardrails applies here too. Insights/Recovery Capital-specific additions:

- **Never frame Recovery Capital as a diagnostic or clinical assessment.** It's a structured self-reflection tool grounded in a real recovery framework — not a medical or psychological evaluation, and never described as one.
- **Keep cadence claims general, not exact.** "More frequent check-ins on Premium" is accurate and durable; citing specific day counts risks going stale if the product changes them.
- **Don't overstate what the AI reads.** The AI-augmented score reads recent journal entries specifically for that one assessment — it isn't a constant background analysis, and it only runs on Premium with the vault unlocked at the time.
- **The domain names are fixed and real — don't rename them in copy.** Health, Home, Purpose, Community. These map to a recognized framework; inventing new domain names for marketing flourish would misrepresent the feature.
